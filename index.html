A RoomTuck project

RoomBunny

RoomBunny is an original educational video project for young audiences. It creates short, age-appropriate explainers and stories with a focus on clear learning, visual safety, and original characters.

Why RoomBunny connects to YouTube

The RoomBunny operator uses Google OAuth to connect the project to its own YouTube channel. The connection is used only to upload RoomBunny videos and read channel analytics needed to understand performance and improve future content.

  • Upload original RoomBunny videos to the authorized YouTube channel.
  • Read YouTube Analytics for that authorized channel.
  • No sale of Google user data.
  • No use of Google user data for advertising or unrelated profiling.

RoomBunny is operated alongside RoomTuck. Access to the OAuth integration is limited to the authorized project operator.
