Terms of Service

These terms cover the RoomBunny project and its Google/YouTube integration operated alongside RoomTuck.

Purpose

RoomBunny is an original educational content project. Its YouTube integration is intended for the authorized project operator to publish RoomBunny videos and review analytics for the connected channel.

Authorized use

The OAuth integration is not offered as a public account-management service. Only the authorized RoomBunny operator should grant access to a Google or YouTube account.

Content and platform rules

RoomBunny content and automation are intended to comply with applicable YouTube and Google policies. Platform availability, API behavior, quotas, and account actions remain subject to Google's and YouTube's own terms and policies.

Availability

The project may change, pause, or discontinue parts of its automated workflow when required for safety, reliability, policy compliance, or platform changes.

Privacy

How Google and YouTube authorization data is handled is described in the RoomBunny Privacy Policy.
